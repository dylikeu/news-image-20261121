One-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026: 42 verified stories across 7 regions, three lead stories with drawn scenes, twelve illustrated briefs, a UTC timeline, a stylised world map with 12 numbered locations, a DATE CHECK box and Friday's market closes. Built to read at thumbnail, half and full size.

Entry for [Sum up 21 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121) on make.fun.

## Run

```sh
python3 render.py news.png
python3 main.py
```

`render.py` draws `news.png` (1024x1024, Pillow only). `main.py` copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`). No network, deterministic.

## What's inside

- Masthead: Saturday, 21 November 2026 — 42 stories, 7 regions, 13 fields.
- The day in UTC: COP31 misses its deadline, Russia's overnight barrage, the UK-EU signing, the cancer-pill results, BepiColombo at Mercury.
- Leads: Russia's barrage hits Kyiv and lands missiles in Poland, COP31 in overtime, UK-EU sign three post-Brexit deals.
- Illustrated briefs: the pancreatic-cancer pill, BepiColombo at Mercury, the FBI database hack, Hormuz tankers, Houthi missiles over Riyadh, the data-centre tax threat, the claimed Riemann proof, GTA 6's $3.8bn day, Europe's cost-of-living protests, Geneva's AI-weapons talks, Denmark's expulsion, Gaza.
- Map: 12 places in the news, with a legend.
- The day in brief: all 42 stories, numbered, each with an evidence tag and source.
- DATE CHECK: every story happened or was first reported on 21 November 2026; the big stories from other dates (ICJ ruling, Shenzhen summit, Anthropic IPO debut, and more) are named as left out, plus what had not happened yet.
- By the numbers: Friday's closes — S&P 500 in correction, Nikkei −5.1%, Brent ~$91.
